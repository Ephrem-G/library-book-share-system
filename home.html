How it works

1

Register & verify

Create an account, then click the verification link we email you so other readers know you are real.

2

List your books

Add the books on your shelf with title, author, category and condition so others can find them.

3

Browse the map

See books available from readers near your location using Google Maps and category filters.

4

Request & chat

Send a borrow request, message the owner, agree on a meet-up and mark the exchange as completed.

Why use Library Book Share?

💸

Save money

Borrow textbooks and novels for free instead of buying every book you only read once.

🌍

Reduce waste

Give the books gathering dust on your shelf a second life with a new reader.

🤝

Meet readers

Connect with people in your neighbourhood who share your reading interests.

🔒

Safe by design

Email verification, JWT-protected APIs and clear borrow/return tracking.
